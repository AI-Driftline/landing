Anthropic retires claude-sonnet-4-5 on 30 November 2026: what to change

13 November 2026 · Driftline

On 30 November 2026, Anthropic removes claude-sonnet-4-5 from the API, together with its dated version. From that day, API calls to it fail. Anthropic's recommended replacement (as of 14 November 2026): claude-sonnet-5-5.

What retires, and what replaces it

Anthropic lists this model on its deprecations page. The dates and replacements on this page come from Driftline's registry, which is checked against that page several times a day, so they stay current if Anthropic changes them.

claude-sonnet-4-5 is the short name for claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929, so both stop working.

Test before 30 November 2026

A new model answers the same prompt differently: format, length, tone, and whether JSON still parses. Take 10 to 20 real prompts from your logs, run each on the old model and the replacement, and compare. Do it while the old model still answers; after the date there is nothing left to compare against.
