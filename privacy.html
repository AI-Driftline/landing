Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Short version

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The Driftline Action runs in your own pipeline and sends me nothing. When you buy Pro, I receive the order details Lemon Squeezy shares with me, and I use them only to run your subscription and answer your questions.

This website

The site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts are served from this site, not from Google. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and abuse prevention; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The Driftline Action

The Action runs inside your GitHub Actions job. Your code, prompts and API keys stay in your pipeline. Prompts go directly from your runner to OpenAI, Anthropic or Google. If you use Pro, the Action sends your license key, and nothing else, to Lemon Squeezy's license API to check that it is valid.

Purchases

Lemon Squeezy processes payments as the merchant of record and handles your card details; I never see them. From Lemon Squeezy I receive your name, email address, country and order details. I use these to issue and manage your license, send service emails about your subscription, and answer support requests. I do not sell or share this data. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy for how they handle it.

Email

If you write to hello@aidriftline.com, I keep the conversation so I can help you and follow up.

Your choices

You can ask me to show, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you by emailing hello@aidriftline.com. I will answer within 30 days. Some order records must be kept for tax reasons; Lemon Squeezy keeps those as merchant of record.
