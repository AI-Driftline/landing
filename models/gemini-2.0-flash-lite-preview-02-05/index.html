Model retirement dates / Google

gemini-2.0-flash-lite-preview-02-05

Google listed 9 December 2025 as the earliest shutdown date for gemini-2.0-flash-lite-preview-02-05. That date has passed, so gemini-2.0-flash-lite-preview-02-05 may stop working at any time. The recommended replacement is gemini-3.1-flash-lite (which is itself retiring; move to gemini-3.5-flash-lite).

Before you switch

A replacement model answers the same prompt differently: tone, length, format, and whether JSON still parses. Driftline Pro runs your real prompts on gemini-2.0-flash-lite-preview-02-05 and gemini-3.5-flash-lite side by side in CI and shows what changed. See a drift test.
