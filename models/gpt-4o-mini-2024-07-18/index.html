Before you switch

A replacement model answers the same prompt differently: tone, length, format, and whether JSON still parses. Driftline Pro runs your real prompts on gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18 and gpt-6-luna side by side in CI and shows what changed. See a drift test.

Get an email before it retires

One email 30 days before, one 7 days before and one on the day, plus a note if OpenAI changes the date. Nothing else. One-click unsubscribe.
